2aMonth-End Management Pack - "Measured Close"

Design reference - Ashcombe Partners

Five page archetypes for a finance month-end management pack. Fictional client: Ashcombe Partners, a professional-services firm reporting a single-month close. Canvas is 1280x720 (Power BI default). Numbers on these mockups are illustrative placeholders; in the built report every figure traces to the committed data generator. Numbered dots map to the notes beside each page.

Design system at a glance

Palette - same measure = same colour, everywhere
#EDF1F0Page canvas - soft neutral, never pure white
#FFFFFFVisual cards, 6px radius, hairline border
#146B60Teal = Actuals (the data ink)
#123F39Deep teal = headings, P&L totals
#B08542Brass = Budget baseline only (markers, dashed)
#2E7D55Green = favourable variance only
#B0402FRed = unfavourable variance only
rampSequential teal ramp for matrix heat
Type - Segoe UI (Power BI native)
Report title 20px semibold
4.28m KPI number 28-30px bold
Visual title 11px caps 700
Axis / table body 10-11px, tabular figures, secondary #5C6B70
Signature rules
  • Colour means favourable / unfavourable, never raw + / -. An under-spend on cost is green; a revenue miss is red.
  • Every actual reads against its budget: a brass baseline marker on bars, a brass line on trends, or a full variance bridge.
  • The profit bridge (waterfall) is the recurring signature move: budget to actual, one driver per step.
  • 24px margins, 8px grid. Fixed bands: title + slicers, KPI band (~104px), then content rows. Tabular figures, right-aligned money.

Month-end scorecard - the 10-second read

1Title band with data currency ("June 2026 close", "figures in £000"). Slicers top-right, same order on every page: Period, Service line, Entity.
2KPI cards carry the month value, the budget, and a favourable/unfavourable delta. Teal top accent; the number is the headline, the context line does the comparison.
3The alarm card. Red on exactly one thing - the driver management must act on. Here, the biggest revenue miss.
4The profit bridge is the signature move: budget op. profit to actual, one step per driver, green favourable and red unfavourable. It explains the miss in one glance.
5Actual bars with a brass budget marker - the eye reads over/under budget without a second series competing for attention.

Profit bridge - the "why" page

1The bridge at full width, decomposed to the individual drivers: each service line's revenue variance, then cost categories. Anchors in brass, favourable green, unfavourable red - the shape tells the story before any number is read.

P&L matrix - the register finance works from

1The management P&L as a matrix: subtotal lines (Revenue, Gross profit, Operating profit) in deep teal, drill lines indented and muted. Expandable to service line, then cost centre.
2Variance % is the only shaded column - a diverging green-to-red scale keyed on favourable/unfavourable, not raw sign. A cost line over budget shades red even though its number is positive.
3Right-click any line to drill through to its detail page. Money right-aligned with tabular figures; brackets for costs, the accountant's convention.
