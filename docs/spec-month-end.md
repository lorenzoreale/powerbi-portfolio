# Spec: Month-End Management Pack

Project 2 of the portfolio. Fictional client: Ashcombe Partners, a professional-services firm reporting a single-month management close. All data is synthetic, generated by a committed deterministic script (Phase 2). This spec is the ground truth for the data generator, the semantic model and the report. Deviations found during build are written back here.

Design: `docs/design-reference-month-end.html`, set **2a** ("Measured Close": soft neutral canvas #EDF1F0, white cards, teal #146B60 for Actuals, brass #B08542 for Budget baseline only, green #2E7D55 favourable and red #B0402F unfavourable). The figures in the mockups are illustrative; every number in the built report must trace to the generator output.

Status: **draft, awaiting Lorenzo's approval**. No code until approved.

## 1. Business scenario

Ashcombe Partners is a mid-market professional-services firm with four client-facing service lines (Advisory, Managed Services, Projects, Support) and a central overhead function. Each month the finance team closes the management accounts and produces a board pack: revenue and profit against budget, the drivers of the variance, the drillable P&L, and the client portfolio behind the revenue.

The report answers four management questions, one per page, plus a drillthrough detail:

1. **Month-End Scorecard**: did we hit the number this month, and where is the variance?
2. **Profit Bridge**: what drove operating profit from budget to actual?
3. **P&L Matrix**: the full management P&L, drillable to service line and account.
4. **Client Portfolio**: where does revenue come from, and at what margin?
5. **Service Line Detail**: one service line end to end (drillthrough target).

The pack reports the selected month against its budget, with a **year-to-date column** alongside so the cumulative position is visible on the P&L. There is no prior-year or rolling-trend framing in the report (see section 9, out of scope).

## 2. Reporting period and current month

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Data range | Jan 2025 to Jun 2026 (18 monthly periods), both Actual and Budget |
| Current month | **Jun 2026**, the latest period with actuals. The report's Period slicer defaults to it |
| Year to date | Jan 2026 to the selected month (Jun 2026 by default). Financial year = calendar year |
| Grain of time | Calendar month. No daily date table: YTD is computed at month grain by filtering the Calendar, so no mark-as-date-table or daily time-intelligence is needed |
| Default report view | Period = Jun 2026, Service Line = All, Entity = Group |

The published figures are deterministic because the Period slicer defaults to a fixed month (Jun 2026), exactly as Project 1 froze its as-at date; YTD is derived from that selected month. Nothing keys off the system clock.

## 3. The core modelling decision: signed amounts

**Every amount is stored as a profit contribution: revenue positive, all costs negative.** This single decision makes the whole pack coherent:

- `SUM(Amount)` at any level of the P&L is the profit at that level. Revenue (+) plus Direct costs (-) equals Gross profit; plus Overheads (-) equals Operating profit.
- **Variance = Actual - Budget carries the favourable/unfavourable sign automatically for every line.** Revenue below budget gives a negative variance (unfavourable, red). A cost under budget: actual is a smaller negative than budget, so Actual - Budget is positive (favourable, green). An overhead over budget gives a negative variance (unfavourable, red). No per-line-type logic is needed; the colour rule is simply `Variance >= 0` favourable, `< 0` unfavourable, everywhere.

This is the finance-literate move the design is built around, and it is why the report never confuses "positive number" with "good".

### Seeded narrative (the story the data must tell)

Targets for the Phase 2 generator, so the report has a true story. Exact values come from the generator; the spec fixes the shape and the Jun 2026 landing figures (in £000, Group, Actual vs Budget):

- **Revenue 4,281 vs 4,500 (-219, unfavourable).** The month missed the top line.
- By service line: Advisory 1,180 / 1,275; Managed Services 1,760 / 1,900; Projects 890 / 860 (favourable); Support 451 / 465.
- **Direct costs (2,646) vs (2,700) (+54 favourable)** and **Overheads (1,023) vs (1,080) (+57 favourable)**: costs were well managed.
- **Gross profit 1,635 vs 1,800 (38.2% vs 40.0%)**; **Operating profit 612 vs 720 (14.3% vs 16.0%, -108 unfavourable)**. The story: strong cost discipline recovered only about half of the revenue shortfall.

- **Client concentration**: a top client (Peverel Group ~640) on plan; a high-revenue low-margin client (Oakline Foods ~415 at ~12%) as the margin-watch item.
- Prior months (Jan 2025 onward) trend gently towards these June figures so budget-vs-actual is realistic in every period, even though only the selected month is shown.

## 5. Star schema

```
Calendar 1--* Ledger *--1 Scenario
                     *--1 Entity
                     *--1 Service Line
                     *--1 Account
                     *--1 Client
Calendar 1--* Capacity *--1 Scenario
                       *--1 Service Line

P&L Line   (disconnected: drives the P&L matrix layout, read by a measure)
```

Six dimensions (Calendar, Scenario, Entity, Service Line, Account, Client) and two facts (Ledger, Capacity). All relationships single-direction, many-to-one from fact to dimension. No inactive relationships are expected. One further table, **P&L Line**, is a disconnected presentation table (section 5.9): it drives the statement layout of the P&L matrix and has no relationship to the facts.

### 5.1 Calendar (dimension, month grain)

Grain: one row per month. Columns: `MonthKey` (int YYYYMM), `Month` (label "Jun 2026", sorted by `MonthKey`), `Month Start` (date), `Quarter`, `Year`, `Financial Period` (e.g. "P06 2026").

### 5.2 Scenario (dimension)

Grain: one row per scenario. Columns: `Scenario` (Actual / Budget). Two rows. Drives the Actual and Budget measures.

### 5.3 Entity (dimension)

Grain: one row per legal entity. Columns: `EntityKey`, `Entity Name`, `Entity Short`. Three rows. "Group" is the slicer's All state, not a stored member.

### 5.4 Service Line (dimension)

Grain: one row per service line. Columns: `ServiceLineKey`, `Service Line`, `Is Client Facing` (Advisory / Managed Services / Projects / Support = true; Central = false).

### 5.5 Account (dimension)

Grain: one row per P&L account. Columns: `AccountKey`, `Account` (leaf name), `Category` (Revenue / Direct costs / Overheads), `Sort Order` (statement order), `Is Cost` (bool). The three categories plus the signed-amount convention (section 3) produce the P&L subtotals.

### 5.6 Client (dimension)

Grain: one row per client. Columns: `ClientKey`, `Client Name`, `Client Since` (year), plus an explicit `Unallocated` member (key 0) for overhead rows so non-client amounts never hide in a blank axis label.

### 5.7 Ledger (fact)

Grain: **one row per Month x Scenario x Entity x Service Line x Account x Client with a non-zero amount.**

| Column | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `MonthKey` | int | FK to Calendar |
| `ScenarioKey` | int | FK to Scenario |
| `EntityKey` | int | FK to Entity |
| `ServiceLineKey` | int | FK to Service Line |
| `AccountKey` | int | FK to Account |
| `ClientKey` | int | FK to Client (Unallocated for overheads) |
| `Amount` | decimal | **Signed profit contribution**: revenue positive, costs negative. Stored in £ (not £000); the report formats to £000 |

### 5.8 Capacity (fact)

Grain: **one row per Month x Scenario x Service Line.** Columns: `MonthKey`, `ScenarioKey`, `ServiceLineKey`, `Billable Hours`, `Available Hours`. Supports Utilisation %. Central has no capacity rows.

### 5.9 P&L Line (disconnected presentation table)

Grain: **one row per line of the management P&L, in statement order.** Columns: `LineKey`, `P&L Line` (label: Revenue, Advisory fees, ... Direct costs, Gross profit, Overheads, Admin staff, ... Operating profit), `Sort Order`, `Line Type` (group total / account detail / subtotal), and `Category Filter` (which account category or categories the line sums; blank for the cumulative subtotals Gross profit and Operating profit). No relationship to the facts: a measure reads the row in context and returns the right amount. This gives full control over where Gross profit and Operating profit appear as rows between their account groups, which a plain account hierarchy cannot do. This structure-table-plus-measure pattern is the chosen approach; it is the finance-grade way to lay out a P&L in Power BI and is part of what this project demonstrates.

## 6. Measure catalogue

Business definitions are binding; exact DAX is Phase 3. Money formats to £000 with no decimals (thousands separator); percentages one decimal place; ratios return BLANK when the denominator is empty. Amounts are signed profit contributions (section 3).

### Scenario values

| # | Measure | Business definition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Actual | Sum of `Amount` where Scenario = Actual, in filter context |
| 2 | Budget | Sum of `Amount` where Scenario = Budget |
| 3 | Variance | Actual minus Budget. Positive = favourable, negative = unfavourable, for every line (section 3) |
| 4 | Variance % | Variance divided by the absolute value of Budget, so the percentage keeps the favourable/unfavourable sign. BLANK when Budget is 0 (see wrinkle W1) |
| 5 | Is Favourable | Helper flag: Variance >= 0. Drives the single green/red conditional-format rule used on every variance visual |

### Period framing (month and YTD)

The base measures (Actual, Budget, Variance) respect the Period slicer, so with the default selection they report Jun 2026. Each has a year-to-date sibling that expands the period to the financial year so far.

| # | Measure | Business definition |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | YTD Actual | Actual summed over the months from January of the selected year to the selected month |
| 7 | YTD Budget | Budget over the same year-to-date window |
| 8 | YTD Variance | YTD Actual minus YTD Budget, same favourable/unfavourable sign convention |

Computed at month grain by filtering `Calendar` to `Year = SELECTEDVALUE(Year)` and `MonthKey <= MAX(MonthKey)`; no daily date table or DAX time-intelligence function is required. The P&L subtotals below each get YTD variants the same way, for the Year-to-date column group on the matrix.

### P&L subtotals

Rendered through the **P&L Line** structure table (section 5.9): a single measure `[P&L Value]` reads the line in context and returns the amount for the mapped account category, with Gross Profit and Operating Profit cumulative. This puts the subtotal rows in the right statement positions.

| # | Measure | Business definition |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | Revenue | Sum of Amount for Category = Revenue |
| 10 | Direct Costs | Sum of Amount for Category = Direct costs (negative) |
| 11 | Gross Profit | Revenue plus Direct Costs |
| 12 | Overheads | Sum of Amount for Category = Overheads (negative) |
| 13 | Operating Profit | Gross Profit plus Overheads |
| 14 | Gross Margin % | Gross Profit divided by Revenue |
| 15 | Operating Margin % | Operating Profit divided by Revenue |
| 16 | P&L Value | Reads the current `P&L Line` row and returns its amount (account-detail lines sum their account; subtotal lines are cumulative). The measure the matrix puts in each Actual / Budget / Variance / YTD cell |

The P&L matrix (page 3) renders these statement lines in order, with a **This month** column group (Actual / Budget / Variance) and a **Year to date** column group (Actual / Budget / Variance), plus a shaded month Variance % and a % of revenue column.

### KPI and headline

| # | Measure | Business definition |
|---|---|---|
| 17 | Revenue vs Budget | Revenue Variance, for the scorecard delta |
| 18 | Operating Profit vs Budget | Operating Profit Variance |
| 19 | Utilisation % | Billable Hours divided by Available Hours (Actual scenario) |
| 20 | Utilisation Target % | Billable divided by Available for Budget scenario |
| 21 | Largest Adverse Variance | The label of the P&L line or service line with the most negative Variance, for the alarm card |

### Client portfolio

| # | Measure | Business definition |
|---|---|---|
| 22 | Client Revenue | Revenue at client grain |
| 23 | Client Gross Profit | Revenue plus Direct Costs at client grain (direct costs carry a client) |
| 24 | Client Margin % | Client Gross Profit divided by Client Revenue |
| 25 | Client Revenue Variance | Client Revenue Actual minus Budget |

### Data quality

| # | Measure | Business definition |
|---|---|---|
| 26 | Missing Budget Lines | Count of account/service-line combinations with actuals but no budget in the month (wrinkle W1) |
| 27 | Unmapped Client Amount | Actual revenue whose source client did not match the client master before cleaning (wrinkle W3), expected small |

## 7. Deliberate data-quality wrinkles

Each is planted by the generator, documented in the generator README, and visibly handled by the model.

| # | Wrinkle | Where | How the model handles it |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | One service line has no budget loaded for a single account in Jun 2026 | budget rows | Variance % guards against divide-by-zero (BLANK, shown as "no budget"), and the Missing Budget Lines measure surfaces the count |
| W2 | A late-journal batch is duplicated in the raw actuals export (~6 rows) | actuals CSV | Deduped on the natural key in a named Power Query step; row counts noted in the README |
| W3 | ~12 revenue rows carry a client name with trailing space or wrong casing | actuals CSV | Trimmed and resolved to `ClientKey` against the client master; any unmatched surfaced by Unmapped Client Amount |
| W4 | Overhead rows have no client | actuals CSV | Mapped to the explicit Unallocated member (key 0), never a blank axis label |
| W5 | One cost is posted with the wrong sign (a positive cost) in the raw export | actuals CSV | Load coerces cost accounts to negative by Category, and the deviation is documented so the correction is visible, not silent |
| W6 | A handful of tiny sub-£1 rounding rows exist in £ | actuals CSV | Aggregation to £000 absorbs them; sum-of-parts still reconciles to the total, demonstrated in the README |

## 8. Report pages

Canvas 1280x720, layout, palette and typography per design set 2a. Colour discipline: Actuals = teal #146B60, Budget = brass #B08542 baseline only, favourable = green #2E7D55, unfavourable = red #B0402F, red/green reserved strictly for variance. Same measure, same colour, every page.

### Slicers (top-right, same order everywhere, synced)

| Slicer | Pages | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Period (month dropdown) | 1, 2, 3, 4 | Jun 2026 |
| Service Line | 1, 2, 3, 4 | All |
| Entity | 1, 2, 3, 4 | All (Group) |

### Page 1: Month-End Scorecard

KPI band (5 cards): Revenue (vs Budget, favourable/unfavourable delta), Gross Margin % (vs budget pts), Operating Profit (vs Budget), Utilisation % (vs target), and a red alarm card naming the largest adverse variance driver. Visuals: operating profit bridge (Budget OP to Actual OP by Revenue / Direct costs / Overheads); P&L summary table (Revenue, Direct costs, Gross profit, Overheads, Operating profit with Actual/Budget/Variance); revenue by service line (actual teal bars with brass budget marker); largest adverse variances table.

### Page 2: Profit Bridge

The full operating-profit waterfall decomposed to individual drivers (each service line's revenue variance, then cost categories), anchors in brass, favourable green, unfavourable red. Supporting: variance by driver category (diverging ranking); favourable/unfavourable split showing the offset.

### Page 3: P&L Matrix

The management P&L as a matrix built on the P&L Line structure table: statement lines (Revenue and its accounts, Direct costs, Gross profit, Overheads and its accounts, Operating profit) down the rows; across the columns a **This month** group (Actual / Budget / Variance) and a **Year to date** group (Actual / Budget / Variance), plus a shaded month Variance % and a % of revenue column. Variance % is the only shaded column, a diverging green-to-red scale on the favourable/unfavourable sign. Expandable to service line then account; brackets for costs; right-aligned tabular figures.

### Page 4: Client Portfolio

Top clients by revenue (actual bars with brass budget marker); margin-vs-revenue scatter (bubble = revenue, the high-revenue low-margin client stands out in red); client register (Client, lead service line, revenue, budget, variance, margin %, status pill). Red reserved for margin-watch and below-plan.

### Page 5: Service Line Detail (drillthrough)

Drillthrough target on Service Line, reachable from the matrix, the service-line bars and the register. Hidden from navigation; back button; filter pill. Header card: service line, revenue, variance, gross margin %, utilisation, operating-profit contribution. Two tables: the line's own P&L (Actual/Budget/Variance) and its clients (revenue, variance, margin).

## 9. Out of scope

- Month and YTD only: no prior-year comparison or rolling-trend/forecast framing (deliberate scope choice; later projects can add temporal depth). YTD is computed at month grain without a daily date table.
- No RLS, no incremental refresh: static CSVs.
- No calculation groups, no field parameters, no composite models: this project shows a clean scenario-based finance model done properly. The signed-amount and Scenario-dimension patterns are the showcase.
- No cash flow or balance sheet: P&L only.
