# Spec: Site Inspection Performance

Project 1 of the portfolio. Fictional client: Meridian Facilities Group, a national facilities and logistics operator running scheduled health, safety and compliance inspections across its UK estate. All data is synthetic, generated by a committed deterministic script (Phase 2). This spec is the ground truth for the data generator, the semantic model and the report. Deviations discovered during build are written back here.

Design: `docs/design-reference-site-inspection.html`, set **1a** (tinted canvas #F3F6FA, white cards, brand blue #24589E, navy #16324F, amber targets, red for bad things only). The figures shown in the mockups are illustrative placeholders; every number in the built report must trace to the generator output.

Status: **draft, awaiting Lorenzo's approval**. No code until approved.

---

## 1. Business scenario

Meridian Facilities Group operates 42 sites (depots, hubs, yards, offices) across six UK regions. A team of inspectors carries out scheduled site inspections against a standard 24-question checklist grouped into six sections. Each question is answered Pass, Fail or N/A. Every Fail is a finding with a severity (High, Medium, Low) and an inspector note. Findings raise corrective actions with an owner, a due date and a closure date once done.

The report answers four management questions, one per page, plus a drillthrough detail:

1. **Executive Overview**: are we inspecting enough, and is compliance where it should be?
2. **Compliance Deep-Dive**: what are we finding, and where does it repeat?
3. **Action Tracker**: are corrective actions getting closed on time?
4. **Coverage**: are we inspecting the right places often enough?
5. **Inspection Detail**: drillthrough target showing one inspection end to end.

## 2. Reporting window and as-at date

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Data range | 1 Jan 2025 to 26 Jun 2026 (18 months) |
| As-at date | **26 Jun 2026**, fixed: the last weekday of the data range (28 Jun 2026 is a Sunday and inspections happen on working days). Defined in the model as `MAX(Inspection[Inspection Date])`, never `TODAY()`. The generator derives it from the scheduled visits, so the generator and the model agree by construction |
| Date table range | 1 Jan 2025 to 30 Jun 2026, marked as date table |
| Default report view | Jan 2026 to Jun 2026 (six whole months to the as-at date) |

Everything time-relative (overdue, days since last inspection, due in next 7 days, MTD, YTD) is computed against the as-at date so the published report is stable and deterministic. `TODAY()`/`NOW()` are banned from the model. YTD means 1 Jan 2026 to the as-at date.

The date slicer is a **month dropdown with a default selection of Jan 2026 to Jun 2026**, not a relative-date slicer: relative slicers key off the real current date and would empty a frozen dataset.

## 3. Entities and volumes

| Entity | Volume | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Regions | 6 | North, Midlands, Scotland, South East, Wales, South West |
| Sites | 42 | Split roughly North 9, Midlands 8, South East 8, Scotland 7, Wales 5, South West 5. Two site types: Logistics and Facilities. Each has lat/long for the map |
| People | 10 | 8 inspectors (one leaves Oct 2025, one joins Feb 2026) plus 2 action owners who never inspect. People can own actions as well as inspect |
| Checklist questions | 24 | 4 per section. Sections: Fire safety, Electrical, Housekeeping, PPE & welfare, Storage, Signage |
| Inspections | ~870 | ~50 per month typical, seasonal dip in December (~35). IDs `SI-YYYY-NNNN` |
| Checklist responses | ~20,900 | 24 rows per inspection |
| Findings | subset of responses | A finding is a response with Result = Fail. Expect roughly 2 to 4 per inspection early in the range, improving over time |
| Actions | ~450 | Raised from every High finding, ~30% of Medium and ~5% of Low. IDs `AC-NNN` |

### Seeded narratives (the story the data must tell)

These are targets for the Phase 2 generator, so the report has something true to say. Exact values come out of the generator; the spec fixes the shape, not the numbers.

- **Compliance improves** from the high 80s (%) in early 2025 to the low 90s by Jun 2026, crossing the 90% target around spring 2026.
- **South West is the weakest region**, a few points below the pack; North is the strongest.
- **Signage is the weakest section** but visibly improving; Electrical is the strongest.
- **Kingsway Depot is the repeat offender**: high inspection volume, findings every month.
- **Neath Yard and Ely Sidings are stale**: no inspection for 70+ days at the as-at date (coverage story).
- **Severity mix shifts away from High** over the window (the deep-dive stacked chart's story).
- **A small overdue tail exists at the as-at date**: around 8 to 12 open actions past their due date, the oldest by roughly a month. Red has something real to point at.

## 4. Star schema

Three fact tables, four dimensions, one target table. All keys are integer surrogates except the human-readable business IDs (`InspectionID`, `ActionRef`), which are kept for display and drillthrough.

```
Date 1--* Inspection *--1 Site
                      *--1 Person (role: inspector)
Date 1--* Action      *--1 Site
                      *--1 Person (role: owner)
                      *--1 Inspection   (via InspectionID, INACTIVE, see below)
Inspection 1--* Response *--1 Question
Date 1--* MonthlyTarget  (month grain)
```

The Inspection-to-Action relationship is **inactive**: Action already relates directly to Site, Person and Date, so an active path through Inspection would create an ambiguous filter diamond, which the engine rejects. A hidden measure (Actions From Inspection, measure 34) activates it with `USERELATIONSHIP` so the Inspection Detail drillthrough can list the actions raised from the inspection in context.

### 4.1 Date (dimension)

Grain: one row per calendar day, 1 Jan 2025 to 30 Jun 2026. Columns: `Date`, `Year`, `Quarter`, `Month Number`, `Month` (e.g. "Jun 2026", sorted by a `YearMonth` key), `Month Start`, `Week Start`, `Day of Week`. Marked as the model's date table.

### 4.2 Site (dimension)

Grain: one row per site. Columns: `SiteKey`, `Site Name`, `Region`, `Site Type` (Logistics / Facilities), `Latitude`, `Longitude`. Names are invented British-sounding depots and hubs (Kingsway Depot, Avonmouth 3, Falkirk Hub, Ely Sidings, Neath Yard, Perth North, Crewe Annex and 35 more).

### 4.3 Person (dimension)

Grain: one row per person. Columns: `PersonKey`, `Person Name` (initial + surname, e.g. "J. Okafor"), `Role` (Inspector / Site manager), `Home Region`. Related twice: to `Inspection[InspectorKey]` and to `Action[OwnerKey]`. Both relationships are active because they land on different fact tables.

### 4.4 Question (dimension)

Grain: one row per checklist question. Columns: `QuestionKey`, `Section`, `Question Text` (human-readable, e.g. "Fire door closes fully unaided"), `Question Code` (e.g. `FS-01`). 24 rows.

### 4.5 Inspection (fact)

Grain: **one row per completed site inspection visit.**

| Column | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `InspectionKey` | int | surrogate |
| `InspectionID` | text | `SI-2026-0341` style, unique, drillthrough key |
| `Inspection Date` | date | FK to Date |
| `SiteKey` | int | FK to Site |
| `InspectorKey` | int | FK to Person |

No score column is stored: the inspection score is the compliance measure evaluated at inspection grain, so header and detail can never disagree.

### 4.6 Response (fact)

Grain: **one row per question answered on an inspection** (24 per inspection).

| Column | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `InspectionKey` | int | FK to Inspection (1-to-many) |
| `QuestionKey` | int | FK to Question |
| `Result` | text | Pass / Fail / NA |
| `Severity` | text | High / Medium / Low when Result = Fail, else blank |
| `Inspector Note` | text | short free text when Result = Fail, else blank |

A **finding** is a Response row with Result = Fail. There is no separate findings table.

### 4.7 Action (fact)

Grain: **one row per corrective action raised.**

| Column | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `ActionRef` | text | `AC-091` style, unique business key (no integer surrogate: nothing relates to it and surrogates on facts waste memory) |
| `InspectionID` | text | FK to Inspection via the inactive relationship (see wrinkle W4: one orphan) |
| `SiteKey` | int | FK to Site, resolved at load |
| `OwnerKey` | int | FK to Person (see wrinkle W5: two unassigned) |
| `Category` | text | the checklist section the finding came from |
| `Raised Date` | date | active relationship to Date |
| `Due Date` | date | no relationship to Date: an inactive one would be activated by no measure, which is orphaned modelling |
| `Closed Date` | date | blank while open; no relationship to Date, same reason |
| `Action Description` | text | short free text |
| `Action Status` | text | **calculated column**: Closed if `Closed Date` present; else Overdue if `Due Date` < as-at date; else Due < 7d if `Due Date` <= as-at date + 7; else Open. Sorted by a hidden helper column so status columns read Open, Due < 7d, Overdue, Closed |

## 5. Targets

### 5.1 MonthlyTarget (fact, month grain)

One row per month: `Month Start`, `Planned Inspections`. Planned volume reflects the estate plan (~52 per month in 2026, slightly lower in 2025, reduced in December). Relationship to Date on `Month Start`.

### 5.2 Static targets (measures, not tables)

- **Compliance target: 90%** (the amber dashed line and KPI context).
- **Closure rate target: 90%** (Action Tracker KPI context).
- **Coverage staleness threshold: 60 days** for the "sites not visited" KPI; **70 days** is the red threshold on the coverage staleness bars, per the design note "a rule, not a vibe".

## 6. Measure catalogue

Business definitions are binding; exact DAX is Phase 3. Formats: whole numbers unless stated; percentages one decimal place. Every ratio measure returns BLANK (not zero) when its denominator is empty, so uninspected cells stay blank in matrices.

### Inspection activity

| # | Measure | Business definition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inspections | Count of inspection visits in filter context |
| 2 | Inspections MTD | Inspections in the as-at month, 1 Jun 2026 to the as-at date |
| 3 | Inspections Previous Month | Inspections in the full month before the as-at month |
| 4 | Inspections MoM Change | Measure 2 minus measure 3, signed |
| 5 | Planned Inspections | Sum of `Planned Inspections` from MonthlyTarget in context |
| 6 | Last Inspection Date | Max inspection date in context; drives the "data to 28 Jun 2026" subtitle |

### Compliance

| # | Measure | Business definition |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | Questions Applicable | Response rows with Result Pass or Fail (N/A excluded from the denominator) |
| 8 | Questions Passed | Response rows with Result = Pass |
| 9 | Compliance % | Passed divided by Applicable. BLANK when nothing applicable. This one measure serves the trend line, the region ranking, the heatmap and the Inspection Detail score, so they reconcile by construction |
| 10 | Compliance % Previous Month | Measure 9 shifted one month back |
| 11 | Compliance MoM (pts) | Measure 9 minus measure 10, in percentage points |
| 12 | Compliance Target % | Static 0.90 |

### Coverage

| # | Measure | Business definition |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | Active Sites | Count of rows in Site (42) |
| 14 | Sites Covered | Distinct sites with at least one inspection in context |
| 15 | Sites Covered Label | Text "37 / 42" style: measure 14 and measure 13 |
| 16 | Days Since Last Inspection | As-at date minus the site's latest inspection date (any period, ignores the date slicer via ALL on Date). BLANK if never inspected |
| 17 | Sites Not Visited 60d | Count of sites whose measure 16 exceeds 60 |
| 18 | Visits YTD | Inspections at the site from 1 Jan 2026 to the as-at date |
| 19 | Latest Compliance % | Compliance % of the site's most recent inspection |

### Findings

| # | Measure | Business definition |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | Findings | Response rows with Result = Fail |
| 21 | Findings per Inspection | Findings divided by Inspections, one decimal place |
| 22 | High Severity Findings | Findings with Severity = High (severity mix visuals slice measure 20 by the Severity column) |

### Actions

| # | Measure | Business definition |
|---|---|---|
| 23 | Actions Raised | Count of actions by Raised Date in context |
| 24 | Actions Raised YTD | Actions raised 1 Jan 2026 to the as-at date |
| 25 | Actions Open | Actions with status Open or Due < 7d (not yet closed, not overdue) |
| 26 | Actions Closed | Actions with status Closed |
| 27 | Closure Rate % | Closed divided by raised, in context. KPI context vs 90% target |
| 28 | Actions Overdue | Actions with status Overdue |
| 29 | Max Days Overdue | Largest (as-at date minus Due Date) among overdue actions |
| 30 | Actions Due Next 7 Days | Actions with status Due < 7d |
| 31 | Median Days to Close | Median of (Closed Date minus Raised Date) over closed actions |
| 32 | Actions per Inspection | Actions raised divided by Inspections in context |

### Data quality and plumbing

| # | Measure | Business definition |
|---|---|---|
| 33 | Orphan Actions | Actions whose InspectionID matches no inspection (expected value: 1, see W4) |
| 34 | Actions From Inspection | Hidden. Activates the inactive Inspection-to-Action relationship so the Inspection Detail page can filter the actions table to the drilled inspection |
| 35 | As-at Date | Hidden. `MAX(Inspection[Inspection Date])` ignoring all filters; the anchor for every time-relative measure |
| 36 | Compliance % Current Month | Hidden. Compliance % of the as-at month; with measure 10 it feeds the MoM KPI delta, so measures 10 and 11 compare the as-at month with the previous full month regardless of the date slicer |

## 7. Deliberate data-quality wrinkles

Each wrinkle is planted by the generator, documented in the generator README, and visibly handled by the model. Nothing is silently ignored.

| # | Wrinkle | Where planted | How the model handles it |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | Coverage gaps: some site-month and section-month combinations have no inspection; two sites stale 70+ days at the as-at date | Inspection scheduling | Blank cells in the deep-dive heatmap (blank means not inspected, never painted as 0%); staleness measures drive the Coverage page |
| W2 | Duplicate export rows: ~5 inspections appear twice in the raw inspections CSV (a re-exported batch), identical `InspectionID` | inspections.csv | Power Query dedupe on `InspectionID`, documented as a named step; row counts before/after noted in the project README |
| W3 | N/A responses: ~4% of checklist answers are N/A | responses.csv | Excluded from the compliance denominator (measure 7); README explains why 24 answers rarely means a denominator of 24 |
| W4 | One orphan action referencing an `InspectionID` that does not exist (a keying error) | actions.csv | Kept, not dropped. Lands on the blank member of the Inspection relationship; surfaced by the Orphan Actions measure and noted in the README |
| W5 | Two actions with a blank owner | actions.csv | Mapped to an explicit "Unassigned" member in Person so they cannot hide in a blank axis label |
| W6 | Dirty site names in the raw actions CSV: trailing whitespace and inconsistent casing on ~10 rows | actions.csv | Trimmed and resolved to `SiteKey` against the site dimension in Power Query, documented as named steps |

## 8. Report pages

Canvas 1280x720, layout, palette and typography exactly per design set 1a. Colour discipline: Inspections volume = brand blue #24589E, Compliance rate = navy #16324F, targets = amber #D9922F dashed, bad things = red #C2453A only, green #3E8E5F sparingly. Same measure, same colour, every page.

### Slicers (top-right, same order everywhere, synced)

| Slicer | Pages | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Date range (month dropdown) | 1, 2, 3, 4 | Jan 2026 to Jun 2026 | Synced across all four |
| Region | 1, 2, 3, 4 | All | Synced across all four |
| Inspector | 1, 2 | All | Person filtered to Role = Inspector |
| Status | 3 only | All | Action Status |
| Reset filters | 1, 2, 3, 4 | n/a | Bookmark restoring defaults |

### Page 1: Executive Overview

KPI band (5 cards): Inspections MTD (vs Planned Inspections, MoM change), Compliance % (MoM pts), Sites Covered Label (with Sites Not Visited 60d context), Closure Rate % ("closed X of Y raised YTD"), Actions Overdue (red card, with Max Days Overdue context).

Visuals: combo chart Inspections (bars, blue) + Compliance % (line, navy, secondary axis) + Compliance Target % (amber dashed) by month; Compliance % by Region (bar, sorted descending, red data label only below target); Findings by Section YTD (bar, sorted); Overdue actions chase list (table: ActionRef, Action Description, Site, Owner, Due Date red).

### Page 2: Compliance Deep-Dive

No KPI band. Visuals: Compliance % matrix Section x Month with sequential blue gradient CF (#D7E3F4 to #24589E), blanks stay blank; Worst questions by fail count YTD (bar, Question Text on axis); Repeat offenders matrix Site x Month with Findings as dot/value (top sites by findings); Finding severity mix by month (100% stacked bar: Low and Medium in blues, High in red).

### Page 3: Action Tracker

KPI band (4 cards): Actions Raised YTD (with Actions per Inspection), Actions Open (with Actions Due Next 7 Days), Closure Rate % (green accent, vs 90% target, with Median Days to Close), Actions Overdue (red, with Max Days Overdue).

Visuals: Actions by Category (bar, sorted); Status by Category matrix (conditional colour on the Overdue column only, intensity by count); Open action register (table sorted by Due Date ascending: ActionRef, Raised, Due, Site, Owner, Status pill, Action Description last).

### Page 4: Coverage

No Inspector slicer. Visuals: map of sites (bubble size = Inspections, colour = Site Type, desaturated base map); Least covered sites by Days Since Last Inspection (bar, red only when over 70 days); Coverage register (table, every site, sorted by Days Since Last Inspection descending: Site, Region, Last visit, Days since, Visits YTD, Latest Compliance %).

Map visual choice (Azure Maps vs standard map vs shape-free alternative) is decided in Phase 4 against what renders reliably in PBIR; if a map proves unreliable the fallback is a scatter of sites by region, and this spec gets updated.

### Page 5: Inspection Detail (drillthrough)

Drillthrough target on `InspectionID`, reachable from any visual carrying it (registers, chase list). Hidden from page navigation. No slicers; back button; filter pill showing the inspection in context. Header card: InspectionID, Site, Date, Inspector, Compliance % (this inspection), Findings count. Two tables: findings on this inspection (Section, Question Text, Severity pill, Inspector Note); actions raised from it (ActionRef, Due, Owner, Status pill, Action Description).

## 9. Out of scope

- No RLS (single-audience demo).
- No incremental refresh, no gateway concerns: static CSVs.
- No composite models, no field parameters, no calculation groups: this project shows fundamentals done properly. Later projects can escalate.
- Inspector league table deliberately omitted: scoring individuals invites the wrong conversation in a portfolio piece; inspector is a slicer, not a ranking.
